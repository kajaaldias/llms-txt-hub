# Gobble List

Gobble List is a family shared grocery-list app: one live list every household member can add to, organized per store, replacing scattered notes and group-chat messages so items stop being missed. Its AI weekly draft suggests what the household is due to buy again.

## Key Focus Areas

- Shared Family Grocery Lists
- AI-Assisted Shopping Drafts
- Household Organization

## About llms.txt Implementation

Gobble List publishes an llms.txt at its root summarizing the product, its key pages, and its blog for families — shared lists, cutting food waste, saving money at the store, and calmer school mornings — refreshed automatically whenever a new post is published.
